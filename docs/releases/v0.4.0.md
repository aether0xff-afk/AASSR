## What changed

### Audited observation contract

The pentest transfer world uses `response_causal_observation_v3`.

The policy snapshot no longer exposes hidden curriculum level, hidden workflow depth, exact session countdown, audit pressure, or other privileged stage configuration. Workflow progress uses a fixed public scale and `goal_progress` is terminal-only.

### ASEQ self-loop rule

ASEQ is the empirical transition tuple `(S,A,S')`.

The guard suppresses an action only after the same semantic self-loop `S -> A -> S` has actually been observed twice. Repeated state-changing transitions remain allowed. If every available action would be guarded, the raw action set is restored.

### Learning-mechanism audit

The predeclared 2x2 separated:

- the historical train-only repetition filter,
- explicit TD episode-boundary handling,
- semantic self-loop ASEQ.

No guided episodes, oracle trajectories, correct-action injection, or intermediate external reward were introduced.

## Development results

Fixed diagnostic matrix: `3 research seeds x 9 levels x 8 diagnostic seeds = 216 episodes` per condition.

| condition | successes | success rate | stalled | truncations | mean final curriculum focus |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `original` | 16/216 | 7.4% | 162 | 6 | 0.00 |
| `original_plus_aseq` | 45/216 | 20.8% | 9 | 97 | 1.67 |
| `learning_fix` | 7/216 | 3.2% | 166 | 7 | 0.00 |
| `learning_fix_plus_aseq` | 31/216 | 14.4% | 12 | 103 | 1.00 |

Across both learning-mechanism backgrounds:

- without ASEQ: `23/432 = 5.3%` success, `328/432 = 75.9%` stalled,
- with ASEQ: `76/432 = 17.6%` success, `21/432 = 4.9%` stalled.

Thus ASEQ increased development diagnostic success by about `+12.3 percentage points` and reduced stalled episodes by about `94%` relative to the no-ASEQ rate.

The ASEQ direction reproduced across every research seed:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 7 | 4 | 14 | 1 | 9 |
| 42 | 7 | 18 | 3 | 9 |
| 100 | 5 | 13 | 3 | 13 |

## Interpretation

The supported mechanism is narrow:

> observation-derived semantic equivalence plus suppression of repeatedly observed empirical `S -> A -> S` self-loops.

The result does not justify suppressing arbitrary repeated transitions.

ASEQ largely converts the previous failure mode from immediate semantic self-loop stalls into continued exploration. The higher truncation count in ASEQ arms shows that unresolved episodes can now continue until other limits rather than repeatedly selecting the same no-progress action.

The historical repetition filter removed `57,691` candidates across the three `original` cells, but removed `0` candidates in `original_plus_aseq`. This supports replacing the broad historical repetition scaffold with the narrower ASEQ self-loop mechanism.

The compound `learning_fix` condition did not improve development success in this experiment. Correct episode-boundary semantics should not be reverted merely for performance; the effect should be diagnosed separately from the now-resolved self-loop mechanism.

## Remaining bottleneck

ASEQ does not solve high-level transfer.

Combined successes by early diagnostic level were:

| level | original | original + ASEQ | learning fix | learning fix + ASEQ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| L0 | 6/24 | 22/24 | 3/24 | 16/24 |
| L1 | 5/24 | 18/24 | 4/24 | 12/24 |
| L2 | 2/24 | 4/24 | 0/24 | 3/24 |
| L3 | 3/24 | 1/24 | 0/24 | 0/24 |
| L4+ | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The next research target is therefore transfer beyond the early curriculum levels, not further broad repetition suppression.

Full AASSR/Imagination remains outside this primary 2x2 until its consequence gate and Prophecy are redesigned/retrained against the audited observation contract.
